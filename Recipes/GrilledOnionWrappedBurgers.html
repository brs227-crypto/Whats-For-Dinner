Grilled Onion Wrapped Burgers

Prep Time
15mins
Cook Time
20mins
Total Time
35mins
Servings
2

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 2 large yellow onions
  • 2 slices cheddar cheese
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ¾ tsp paprika
  • ¼ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped pickles
  • 1 tsp pickle juice
  • ½ tsp white vinegar
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Directions

Step 1: Peel onions and slice into thick rings, about ½ inch wide. Carefully separate the rings, keeping the largest ones intact to use as the burger wraps.

Step 2: Divide ground beef into 2 portions and shape into patties. Season with garlic powder, ½ tsp paprika, salt, and pepper.

Step 3: Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add onion rings and cook for 4–5 minutes per side until softened and golden brown. Remove from pan.

Step 4: In the same pan, cook burger patties for 4–5 minutes per side, or until fully cooked (160°F internally). Add cheddar cheese during the last minute and let melt.

Step 5: While burgers cook, mix Greek yogurt, ketchup, mustard, chopped pickles, pickle juice, vinegar, ¼ tsp paprika, salt, and pepper in a small bowl until smooth.

Step 6: Place each burger patty between two large grilled onion rings instead of a traditional bun.

Step 7: Serve with the homemade In-N-Out style Greek yogurt sauce for dipping and extra pickles if desired!
