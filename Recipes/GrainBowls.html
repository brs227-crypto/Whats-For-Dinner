Grain Bowls

Prep Time
15mins
Cook Time
30mins
Total Time
45mins
Servings
2

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 cup dry quinoa (or rice)
  • 1 can chickpeas (15 oz), drained
  • 1 medium sweet potato
  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 red onion
  • 1/2 cup hummus
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Any additional dips or dressings you like

Directions

Step 1: Preheat oven to 425°F. Cook quinoa according to package instructions, or substitute with rice.

Step 2: Dice sweet potato, chop broccoli, and slice red onion. Toss with olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper. Spread on a baking sheet and roast for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway through, until tender.

Step 3: Drain, rinse, and thoroughly dry chickpeas. Toss with olive oil, paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper. For the air fryer, cook at 390°F for 12–15 minutes, shaking halfway. For the oven, roast at 425°F for 20–30 minutes, shaking halfway, until crispy.

Step 4: Season chicken breasts with olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Heat a pan over medium heat and cook for 5–7 minutes per side, depending on thickness, until the internal temperature reaches 165°F. Let rest before slicing.

Step 5: Assemble bowls with a base of quinoa or rice, roasted sweet potatoes, broccoli, onions, chickpeas, and sliced chicken.

Step 6: Top with a generous scoop of hummus and any additional dips or dressings you like.
