Elote Corn Beef Bowls

Prep Time
10mins
Cook Time
25mins
Total Time
35mins
Servings
2

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes
  • 1½ cups corn (fresh, frozen, or canned)
  • ½ cup cottage cheese
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • ¼ cup crumbled cotija cheese (optional)
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Directions

Step 1: Preheat oven to 425°F. Peel and dice sweet potatoes into small cubes. Toss with olive oil, ½ tsp paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Spread on a baking sheet and roast for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway.

Step 2: Heat a pan over medium heat. Add ground beef and season with cumin, ½ tsp paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Cook for 8–10 minutes, breaking apart the beef until fully browned and cooked through.

Step 3: Blend cottage cheese until smooth and creamy.

Step 4: Heat corn in a pan over medium heat for 4–5 minutes until warm and slightly golden.

Step 5: In a bowl, mix corn with blended cottage cheese, mayonnaise, chili powder, juice of ½ lime, and cotija cheese (optional).

Step 6: Divide roasted sweet potatoes and ground beef between two bowls. Add a generous scoop of creamy elote corn and finish with a squeeze of lime.
