Crispy Rice and Spicy Tuna

Prep Time
10mins
Cook Time
15mins
Total Time
25mins
Servings
2 (4 Tacos)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked sushi rice
  • ½ lb sushi-grade tuna, diced
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise (preferably Kewpie)
  • 1 tbsp sriracha
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Sesame seeds or green onions (optional)

Directions

Step 1: Rinse sushi rice and cook according to package instructions. Mix with rice vinegar, sugar, and a pinch of salt.

Step 2: Spread cooked rice into a parchment-lined container, pressing down firmly into an even layer about 1 inch thick. Refrigerate for 1–2 hours until completely cooled and firm.

Step 3: Cut chilled rice into 8 small rectangles. Lightly brush each piece with olive oil and sprinkle with garlic powder.

Step 4: Preheat air fryer to 400°F. Place rice pieces in a single layer and air fry for 15–20 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crispy.

Step 5: While rice cooks, dice sushi-grade tuna into small cubes.

Step 6: In a bowl, mix tuna with mayonnaise, sriracha, soy sauce, and sesame oil until evenly coated. Keep refrigerated until serving.

Step 7: Remove crispy rice from the air fryer and let cool slightly.

Step 8: Top each crispy rice piece with a generous spoonful of spicy tuna. Garnish with sesame seeds or green onions if desired.
