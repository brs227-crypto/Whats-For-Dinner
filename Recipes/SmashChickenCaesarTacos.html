Smash Chicken Caesar Tacos

Prep Time
10mins
Cook Time
15mins
Total Time
25mins
Servings
2 (4 Tacos)

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground chicken
  • 4 small flour tortillas
  • 2 cups chopped romaine lettuce
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ¼ cup Caesar dressing
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp Italian seasoning
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Lemon juice (optional)

Directions

Step 1: Season ground chicken with garlic powder, paprika, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Mix until combined.

Step 2: Divide chicken into 4 equal portions. Spread a thin, even layer of chicken onto one side of each tortilla, pressing it down firmly.

Step 3: Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Place tortillas chicken-side down and cook for 4–5 minutes until golden brown and chicken is fully cooked (165°F internally).

Step 4: Flip tortillas and cook for another 1–2 minutes until the tortilla is lightly crispy.

Step 5: In a bowl, toss chopped romaine lettuce with Caesar dressing, Parmesan cheese, and a squeeze of lemon juice if desired.

Step 6: Remove tacos from the pan and top each with a generous scoop of Caesar salad.

Step 7: Serve immediately while the chicken is warm and the tortillas are crispy!
