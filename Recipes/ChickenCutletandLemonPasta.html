Chicken Cutlet and Lemon Pasta

Prep Time
15mins
Cook Time
25mins
Total Time
40mins
Servings
2

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts, sliced into thin cutlets
  • 8 oz rotini pasta
  • 1 shallot, finely chopped
  • 1 lemon (juice and zest)
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ¼ cup heavy cream
  • ½ cup Italian breadcrumbs
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp Italian seasoning
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Directions

Step 1: Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Cook pasta according to package instructions. Reserve ½ cup pasta water before draining.

Step 2: Season chicken cutlets with garlic powder, paprika, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.

Step 3: Set up three bowls with flour, beaten egg, and breadcrumbs. Coat each chicken cutlet in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs.

Step 4: Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook chicken for 4–5 minutes per side until golden, crispy, and cooked through (165°F internally). Remove from pan and slice into strips.

Step 5: In a separate pan, melt butter over medium heat. Add chopped shallots and cook for 2–3 minutes until softened.

Step 6: Add heavy cream, lemon juice, lemon zest, and Parmesan cheese. Stir until smooth, adding a little reserved pasta water to thin the sauce if needed.

Step 7: Add cooked pasta to the sauce and toss until evenly coated. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

Step 8: Divide lemon pasta between two bowls and serve with sliced crispy chicken cutlets.
