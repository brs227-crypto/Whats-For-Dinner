Greek Bowls

Prep Time
15mins
Cook Time
20mins
Total Time
35mins
Servings
2

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 cup dry quinoa or Mediterranean grain blend
  • 1 tomato
  • ½ cucumber
  • ¼ red onion
  • ¼ cup crumbled feta cheese
  • ½ cup hummus
  • ½ cup tzatziki
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Directions

Step 1: Cook quinoa or grain blend according to package instructions, usually 15–20 minutes.

Step 2: Season chicken breasts with olive oil, garlic powder, oregano, paprika, salt, pepper, and a squeeze of lemon juice.

Step 3: Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook chicken for 5–7 minutes per side, depending on thickness, until golden brown and cooked through (165°F internally). Let rest for 5 minutes before slicing.

Step 4: Dice tomato, cucumber, and red onion. Toss together with crumbled feta, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.

Step 5: Divide cooked grains between two bowls. Add sliced chicken and Greek salad.

Step 6: Finish with a scoop of hummus and tzatziki. Serve and enjoy!
