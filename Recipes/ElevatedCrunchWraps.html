Elevated Crunch Wraps

Prep Time
15mins
Cook Time
20mins
Total Time
35mins
Servings
2

Ingredients

  • ½ lb lean ground beef
  • 2 large flour tortillas
  • 2 small flour tortillas
  • 2 tostada shells
  • ½ bell pepper
  • ½ red onion
  • ½ cup shredded lettuce
  • ½ cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • ¼ cup nacho cheese sauce
  • ½ tomato, diced
  • ¼ cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp taco seasoning
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Directions

Step 1: Slice bell pepper and onion into thin strips. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat and cook for 5–7 minutes until softened and slightly golden. Remove from pan.

Step 2: In the same pan, add ground beef and season with taco seasoning, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper. Cook for 7–10 minutes, breaking apart the beef until fully browned.

Step 3: Warm the large flour tortillas for a few seconds to make them easier to fold.

Step 4: Place ground beef in the center of each large tortilla. Add nacho cheese sauce, grilled peppers, and onions.

Step 5: Place a tostada shell on top, then layer with Greek yogurt, shredded lettuce, diced tomato, and shredded cheddar cheese.

Step 6: Place a small tortilla on top and fold the edges of the large tortilla toward the center, covering the filling.

Step 7: Heat a lightly oiled pan over medium heat. Place crunchwrap seam-side down and cook for 2–3 minutes per side until golden brown and crispy.

Step 8: Slice in half and serve warm!
